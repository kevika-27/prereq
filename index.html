Topological course planner

Models the NYU Courant course catalog as a directed acyclic graph. Tick off what you have finished, and it computes the shortest valid path to the degree using Kahn's algorithm, packs each semester against a real credit budget, and finds the critical path that sets your true minimum time to graduate.

NYU requires 16 and caps at 18 without an overload petition. Most CSCI-UA and MATH-UA courses are 4 credits, so that is four to five courses.
